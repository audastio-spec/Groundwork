Frequently asked questions

Does my inquiry reserve a car?

No. We are collecting interest before buying our first cars. There are no available vehicles or confirmed reservations yet.

What will the rental cost?

Rates have not been announced. Before renting after launch, request a written quote for your dates. We will explain the rental rate, applicable taxes and fees, deposit, mileage allowance, and payment and cancellation terms before you decide.

Where is pickup?

Contact us to arrange pickup in Winter Haven. Confirm the location and time with us before traveling. Airport pickup and delivery should not be assumed.

What insurance do I need?

Ask us to confirm the insurance requirements and coverage for the specific vehicle and intended use. A personal policy or a platform account alone does not establish coverage for a rental.

Can I drive for Uber, Lyft, or a delivery app?

Tell us the exact platform and service you intend to use. Rental-program approval, vehicle eligibility, driver eligibility, and insurance must all be confirmed. We do not promise that every rental can be used on every platform.
