What you get and what you do not

We believe in being straightforward. Here is exactly what is covered and what is not.

Included in your weekly rate

  • Scheduled maintenance, oil changes, and tire rotations
  • 24/7 roadside assistance
  • Current vehicle registration
  • commercial rental fleet auto liability coverage
  • Insurance and registration documents for platform vehicle inspections

Not included

  • Fuel (you pay for your own gas)
  • Tolls (SunPass, E-Pass, or cash tolls are your responsibility)
  • Traffic citations, parking tickets, or red-light camera fines
  • Personal-use coverage beyond the stated liability policy (consider your own supplemental insurance)
  • Comprehensive or collision coverage (unless separately arranged)

Frequently asked questions

Do you check credit?

No. We do not run a credit check. We verify your driver's license and driving record only. Your credit score has no bearing on your application.

What is the deposit and when do I get it back?

The deposit is $400, due at the time of pickup. It is fully refundable when you return the vehicle in the same condition you received it, minus normal wear and tear. Refunds are typically processed within 15 business days, with an itemized statement of deductions.

Which platforms can I drive for?

Any platform you choose. Our vehicles are used by drivers on Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Amazon Flex, Instacart, Grubhub, Walmart Spark, and others. We do not restrict which platforms you work with, and you can drive for multiple platforms simultaneously.

Will the car pass Uber or Lyft vehicle inspection?

Yes. All our vehicles are maintained to meet or exceed the vehicle requirements for major rideshare platforms, including age, condition, and safety standards. We provide the registration and insurance documents you need for platform vehicle inspections.

What happens if I am in an accident?

Contact us immediately and file a police report at the scene. Our vehicles carry commercial rental fleet auto liability coverage that meets Florida state minimum liability requirements. Depending on the circumstances, your deposit and your personal insurance may also apply. We will walk you through the process step by step. If you are actively on a gig platform trip at the time of the accident, that platform's insurance may also provide coverage.

Can I use the car for personal driving?

Yes, within the mileage policy stated in your rental agreement. The vehicle is yours for the week. You can run personal errands, commute, and handle daily life. Just stay within the mileage terms.

Who pays for maintenance and repairs?

We do. Routine maintenance, oil changes, tire rotations, and mechanical repairs are our responsibility. You are responsible for keeping the vehicle reasonably clean and reporting any mechanical issues, warning lights, or damage promptly.

What if I need to return the car early?

You can return the vehicle at any time. We ask for 7 days' written notice when possible so we can coordinate. Partial-week refunds are handled per the terms in your signed rental agreement.

Is there a mileage cap?

1,500 miles per week included. If you exceed the mileage allowance, excess miles are charged at $0.15 per mile. The exact terms are detailed in your rental agreement.

What documents do I need to bring at pickup?

Bring three things: (1) a valid U.S. driver's license that you have held for at least one year, (2) proof of your active gig platform account (a screenshot of your driver or delivery dashboard is fine), and (3) the deposit payment. That is it. No pay stubs, no bank statements, no credit application.

How do I pay for the weekly rental?

Weekly payments are due every Wednesday. We accept weekly ACH autopay (required), debit card as backup on file. Payment details are outlined in your rental agreement at pickup.
