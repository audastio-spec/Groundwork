Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum rental period?

The minimum rental is 3 days. We offer both daily and weekly rates. Contact us for your specific dates and we will quote the best price.

Do I need a credit check to rent?

No credit check is required. We hold a refundable $500 deposit at pickup, and we verify your license and driving record before confirming your reservation.

Can I use the car to visit LEGOLAND, Disney, and other parks?

Yes. There are no geographic restrictions. Drive to LEGOLAND, Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, Bok Tower Gardens, the beaches, or anywhere else in Florida.

Where do I pick up and return the car?

Pickup and return are in Winter Haven, Florida. We provide the exact location after your reservation is confirmed. Pickup and return are at our Winter Haven location. We do not currently offer airport pickup or delivery.

What is included in the rate?

Your rate includes the vehicle, commercial rental auto liability coverage, current registration, and 24/7 roadside assistance. Fuel, tolls, and traffic citations are your responsibility.

Is there a mileage limit?

200 miles per day included, $0.25 per mile after

What documents do I need to bring?

Bring a valid driver's license held for at least one year and the deposit payment. International visitors must also present a valid passport and, if the license is not in English, an International Driving Permit. Contact us before booking so we can confirm eligibility.

Do you offer airport pickup or delivery?

Pickup and return are at our Winter Haven location. We do not currently offer airport pickup or delivery.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel at least 72 hours before your pickup time for a full deposit refund. Inside 72 hours, one day of the rental rate is retained. No-shows forfeit one day of the rate. Refunds are returned within 10 business days.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes, subject to availability. Let us know as soon as you think you will need extra days and we will do our best to keep you in the same vehicle. If that car is reserved for someone else, we will try to offer an alternative.
